**User Testing Guide for Enhanced Rarity Visuals**

Thank you for your patience as we refine these styles! We've made significant changes to make the card rarities more distinct and visually impactful, with prominent border glows and vibrant colors. Please pull the latest changes and check the following:

**I. Key Things to Verify for Each Rarity (R, SR, SSR, UR, MR):**

1.  **Border Glow:**
    *   **Visibility:** Is a colored glow clearly visible around the border of SR, SSR, UR, and MR cards? (R cards should not have a glow).
    *   **Color Accuracy:** Does the glow color match the rarity's theme?
        *   SR: Green glow
        *   SSR: Blue glow
        *   UR: Purple glow
        *   MR: Gold/Amber glow
    *   **Intensity/Impact:** Is the glow prominent enough to provide a good visual "pop" for the higher rarities? Does it look like the border is "emitting light"?

2.  **Card Body Color:**
    *   **Distinctiveness:** Does the main background color of the card clearly reflect the rarity's theme?
        *   R: White/very light gray card body.
        *   SR: Clearly green card body.
        *   SSR: Clearly blue card body.
        *   UR: Clearly purple card body.
        *   MR: Clearly gold/amber card body.
    *   **Saturation/Vibrancy:** Are the colors saturated and vibrant enough to make each rarity feel distinct and appealing?

3.  **Text & Title Colors:**
    *   **Legibility:** Is all text (main content, titles, IDs, timestamps, rarity name) clearly readable against the new card backgrounds?
    *   **Contrast:** Is there good contrast between the text and its background for all rarities?
    *   **Title Prominence:** Do the titles for each rarity (especially SR and above) stand out appropriately with their new colors/font weights?

4.  **Overall Impression per Rarity:**
    *   Does each rarity now have a clear and unique visual identity?
    *   Does it feel "美观精致" (beautiful and refined) and impactful?

**II. Specific Rarity Checks:**

*   **R (普通 - White/Light):**
    *   Clean, simple, light appearance. Dark text. No glow.
*   **SR (稀有 - Green):**
    *   Visible green glow. Vibrant green background. Clear text. Prominent green-themed title.
*   **SSR (超稀有 - Blue):**
    *   Visible blue glow (should be slightly more intense than SR). Vibrant blue background. Clear text. Prominent blue-themed title. Pulse animation.
*   **UR (终极 - Purple):**
    *   Visible purple glow (more intense than SSR). Vibrant purple background. Clear text. Prominent purple-themed title. Bounce animation & flair icon.
*   **MR (神话 - Gold/Amber):**
    *   Strongest gold/amber glow and thickest border. Vibrant gold/amber background. Clear text. Very prominent gold/amber title. Transform effect & flair icon. Shimmer animation.

**III. Providing Feedback for Iteration:**

Your feedback is crucial! If some aspects are great but others need tweaking, please be as specific as possible. For example:

*   *"The green glow on SR is good, but the blue glow on SSR is too faint."*
*   *"The background color for UR (purple) is perfect, but the text is hard to read."*
*   *"MR cards look very premium, but the gold glow could be even stronger."*
*   *"R cards are much better now, very clean."*
*   *"Overall, this is a big improvement, but SR and SSR feel too similar in vibrancy."*

This detailed feedback will help me make targeted adjustments if needed. We want to ensure these visuals meet your expectations for impact and refinement.

After you've had a chance to review, please let me know your thoughts!
